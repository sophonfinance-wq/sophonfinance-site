Our approach

The same six stages, every run.

Tax, accounting or audit, each machine follows one method, so a reviewer always knows where to look.

  1. 01

    Read the client’s files

    Trial balance, ledgers, invoices, statements and prior work, opened read-only.

  2. 02

    Do the work

    Match, calculate, sample and test, the same way every run.

  3. 03

    Check against the rules

    Official IRS and state pages, the client’s policy book or the audit plan.

  4. 04

    Trace every figure

    Each amount links to the exact document and cell it came from.

  5. 05

    Package for review

    Workpapers, a draft memo or return, and the list of open items.

  6. 06

    Your team decides

    Nothing is posted, paid, signed or filed until a person approves it.

What you receive

A review-ready package, not a black box.

Every run ends with three things your reviewer can open, check and sign off.

01

Linked Excel workpapers

Summary, findings and draft entries up front, evidence tabs behind them. Every amount is a live formula back to the source cell, so a reviewer can follow any number.

02

A draft memo or return

A PDF your reviewer can read in one sitting: the findings with their rules, the figures, and the open items. Always marked as a draft and unsigned.

03

The evidence trail

The original files, the calculations and a hash-checked record of everything the run relied on, so the package can be re-verified later.

Questions

Frequently asked.

Anything else, ask us in the consultation.

Is the data in the videos real?

No. HANGS Construction, its people, documents and figures are invented for demonstration. The machines, their calculations and the runs you see are real; the videos shorten them.

Does a machine file, post, pay or sign anything?

No. Each machine writes drafts. Open items stay open until a person answers them, and signing and filing stay with your team and your licensed professionals.

How long does a run take?

The real work takes from about a second to under a minute. On screen a run is slowed down so a person can read along, and the real working time is shown separately at the end.

Where does it run?

On a computer you control. Each machine reads the client’s folder read-only and keeps its own records of every run.

Can it work on our own files?

That’s what the consultation is for. We look at one process with you, a filing, the month-end invoice review or an audit area, and scope a first phase.
