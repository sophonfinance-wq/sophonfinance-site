02 · Training video · 3:33

Every vendor invoice, matched to the books before month-end closes.

The machine reads each invoice PDF, matches it to the bill as booked, and checks the full cost of assets, the job and cost coding, the entity, the approver and the vendor file. It explains every dollar of difference between the paper and the books.

03 · Training video · 2:58

Year-end audit workpapers, built from the evidence up.

The machine sets materiality, draws and vouches the sample, searches for unrecorded bills, tests the journal entries and ties cash to the bank’s own confirmation. It then writes workpapers and draft findings for the CPA. It gives no opinion. The CPA concludes.

Real product · invented company · 1080p

The six stages

  • Set materiality from the recomputed trial balance
  • Draw and vouch the sample; search for unrecorded bills
  • Test journal entries; confirm cash and compare the year

What one run produces

  • Workpapers with tickmarks: 14 workpaper tabs and 16 evidence tabs
  • Draft findings PDF, one evidence page per figure
  • A hash-checked record of every file it relied on

What stays with the CPA

Every open item stays open until the CPA decides it. The machine never concludes, posts an adjustment, signs or sends anything.
