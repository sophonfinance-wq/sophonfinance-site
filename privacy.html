Privacy notice

What this website collects, why, and who handles it. In plain words.

What we collect

Only what you type into the contact form:

  • Name and work email
  • Company (optional) and the service you pick
  • Your message

Please don’t put client data, tax IDs or bank details in the form.

We don’t use analytics, advertising pixels or tracking cookies.

Why

To reply to you and set up the consultation you asked for. Nothing else.

Who processes it

  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) receives the form, emails it to us and sends you an automatic confirmation.
  • Our email provider stores the messages we send and receive at contact@sophonfinance.com.
  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.) hosts this site. Like any web host, it sees your IP address and browser details when pages load.
  • Google Fonts (Google LLC) serves the fonts. Your browser requests them from Google, which sees your IP address and browser details.

Each handles data under its own privacy policy.

On your device

The site keeps a small “intro seen” flag in your browser’s session storage, so the intro plays once per visit. It clears when you close the tab.

The demos remember settings such as pace, narration and the sample period in your browser’s local storage. These stay on your device and are not sent to us.

Demos use sample data

Every demo uses fictional companies and sample data only. Demos run in your browser and don’t upload your files.

We don’t sell your data

We don’t sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising.

How long we keep it

We keep consultation emails while we’re in touch with you and for our business records, and delete them when you ask, unless the law requires us to keep them.

Your choices

Email contact@sophonfinance.com to ask what we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. We’ll confirm when it’s done.

Who we are

F1 Finance, Seattle, Washington, USA. This site is for business visitors in the United States and isn’t directed at children.
